# Global Superstore — Sales & Profitability Analysis

An end-to-end data analysis project exploring global retail sales, profitability, customer segments, product categories, discounts, and shipping performance using Python, Pandas, and Tableau.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Global Superstore dataset to understand business performance, identify profit drivers, evaluate discount patterns, and highlight areas that may require further investigation.

The objective is to transform transactional data into actionable business insights through data analysis, KPI reporting, and visualization.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, and profit margin.
- Evaluate annual sales and profitability trends.
- Compare product categories and sub-categories.
- Assess market and customer-segment performance.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profit margins.
- Compare shipping modes and fulfillment performance.
- Identify loss-making transactions and potential improvement areas.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python** — analysis and calculations
- **Pandas** — data cleaning and aggregation
- **Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab** — analytical workflow
- **Tableau** — business intelligence and visualization
- **Microsoft Excel** — dataset exploration

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Sales and Profit Trends
Compare annual sales and profit to understand historical business performance.

### 2. Category and Sub-Category Performance
Identify which product groups contribute most to sales and profit, and which require closer review.

### 3. Market Performance
Compare sales, profitability, and customer reach across markets.

### 4. Customer Segments
Examine sales and profit contributions across customer segments.

### 5. Discount and Profitability Analysis
Compare profit margins across discount bands to identify patterns that warrant further investigation.

### 6. Shipping Performance
Assess sales, profit, and margin by shipping mode.

### 7. Loss-Making Transactions
Identify negative-profit transaction lines for further analysis of discounting, product mix, and fulfillment costs.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate negative-profit transactions by product, market, discount, and shipping mode.
- Evaluate discount policies using profit margin rather than sales growth alone.
- Review lower-margin product groups and markets before making pricing or assortment changes.
- Track sales, profit, margin, order volume, and discount levels in a recurring dashboard.
- Extend the analysis with repeat-purchase and customer-cohort metrics.

These are investigation priorities, not proven causal conclusions.

## Data & Methodology

The analysis uses the supplied Global Superstore workbook, containing 51,290 transaction-line records covering 2011–2014.

- Total sales = sum of Sales.
- Total profit = sum of Profit.
- Aggregate profit margin = total profit / total sales.
- Customer count = distinct customer IDs.
- Order count = distinct order IDs.

## Limitations

- The dataset represents historical business activity, not current performance.
- Discount patterns do not establish causation.
- Currency conventions and accounting definitions should be verified before external financial use.
- Further investigation is required before treating any recommendation as a proven business improvement.
